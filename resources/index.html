Why we publish these

Good websites are built on boring fundamentals. These resources collect the checks we use when we launch and improve client sites.

01

Use them with your team

Turn each list into tasks with an owner and a deadline. A checklist only helps when someone is responsible for it.

02

Adapt them to your risk

A brochure site and a store handling payments need different levels of security. Use the checklist as a baseline, then go deeper where it matters.

03

Measure the result

For conversion work, change one thing at a time and keep a record. Opinions are useful; evidence is better.
