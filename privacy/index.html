Privacy Policy

This page explains what information Midnight Web collects when you visit this site, why we collect it, and what choices you have.

Last updated: September 2026

What we collect

This site is a marketing website for a small web design and hosting studio. We keep the data we collect to a minimum:

  • Contact form submissions. If you reach out through our contact form or email us directly, we receive whatever you type in — typically your name, email address, and a message about your project.
  • Language preference. If you switch the site language (EN/RO/RU), that choice is saved in your browser's local storage so we don't ask you again on your next visit. This never leaves your device.
  • Cookie consent choice. Whether you accepted or declined our cookie banner is saved in your browser's local storage, again so we don't ask you again on your next visit.
  • Job applications. Our Careers page doesn't submit anything to our servers. When you click "Send," it opens your own email app with a message pre-filled, and you choose whether to attach a CV, portfolio, or other files before sending it yourself. We only receive what arrives in that email, the same as any message sent to us directly.

We do not currently run analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking scripts on this site. If that changes in the future, we'll update this page and this notice before anything loads.

Why we collect it

  • Contact details you send us are used solely to respond to your inquiry and, if you become a client, to deliver our services.
  • Language and cookie-consent preferences are used only to make the site work the way you last set it up — nothing more.
  • Anything you send us through a job application email is used solely to review you as a candidate for the role you applied to.

Cookies & local storage

This site does not set tracking or advertising cookies. The only browser storage in use is local storage (not a traditional cookie) for the two preferences described above. You can clear this at any time through your browser's site settings, which will reset your language and reshow the cookie banner.

How long we keep it

Messages sent through our contact form are kept only as long as needed to respond to you or, if you become a client, for the duration of our working relationship plus a reasonable period for our records. Language and cookie-consent preferences live in your own browser and are never transmitted to us. Job applications are kept only as long as needed to consider you for the role (and any other open role you might be a fit for), and are deleted after that unless you ask us to keep them longer.

Who we share it with

We don't sell or rent your information. Contact form submissions may pass through our email provider and, if we build you a site, our hosting infrastructure — both chosen for their own security and privacy practices. We don't share your details with advertisers.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time to:

  • tell you what information we hold about you,
  • correct anything that's inaccurate,
  • delete the information we hold about you, or
  • stop using it for a particular purpose.

To do any of this, just email us at contact.midnightwebs@gmail.com and we'll get back to you.

Contact

Questions about this policy or how we handle your data can be sent to contact.midnightwebs@gmail.com.
