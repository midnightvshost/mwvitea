Careers

Help us build the sites small businesses run on.

Midnight Webs is a small, hands-on studio. We design, host, and secure websites for real businesses, and we're looking for people who care about doing that well. Below are the roles we're hiring for right now.

Open roles

Every role here works closely with the rest of the team. Pick the one that fits, or scroll down if none of them quite do.

Engineering

Web Developer

Build and maintain custom sites for our clients, from first draft to launch, with an eye for clean code, speed, and pixel-level detail.

Client Relations

Clients Manager

Be the main point of contact for our clients, from onboarding through ongoing support, keeping projects on track and clients informed.

Moderation

Server Staff

Manage and maintain the Midnight Webs Discord server, helping members, keeping channels organized, enforcing server rules, and making sure the community stays welcoming and active.

Quality

QA / Web Tester

Test sites across browsers and devices before launch, track down bugs, and help make sure nothing ships broken.

Delivery

Project Manager

Plan timelines, coordinate between design, development, and clients, and make sure every project ships on time and on scope.

Support

Technical Support Specialist

Assist clients with technical issues after launch, help resolve issues involving domains, email, integrations, and other services to keep everything running smoothly.
